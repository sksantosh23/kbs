## Workflow

Material changes move through `RECEIVED → ANALYZED → PLANNED → IMPLEMENTING → VERIFIED → ABSORBED → CLOSED`. Minor reversible engineering refinements remain ordinary technical decisions and are recorded in the Technical Register when material; they do not require a change entry.
